Veyqo

A unified integration layer for payment providers and developer webhooks.

In development — scope evolving

Problem and context

Different payment providers expose different authentication, webhook formats and operational behavior. Veyqo is designed to provide a consistent interface for application teams integrating with several providers.

What I worked on

  • Designed the Laravel backend and orchestrator boundaries around provider connection and event processing.
  • Worked on incoming webhook normalization and outgoing delivery workflows.
  • Configured database-backed queue workers and production email.
  • Planned subscription and transaction-based revenue models.

Technical considerations

  • Validate incoming callbacks before accepting a payment event.
  • Use a canonical event payload to isolate clients from provider-specific fields.
  • Queue outbound deliveries so slow destinations do not block inbound provider requests.
  • Protect provider credentials and operational workflows through access controls.
