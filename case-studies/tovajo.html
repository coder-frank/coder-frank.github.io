Tovajo

Organizer-friendly group workflows with Laravel APIs and notifications.

In development — scope evolving

Problem and context

Organizing a community involves member access, roles, announcements and recurring coordination. Tovajo brings these workflows into one product rather than relying on disconnected channels.

What I worked on

  • Worked on backend architecture and API workflows for group organization.
  • Reviewed organizer permissions, feature design and monetization.
  • Configured production Laravel deployment, SMTP and database queues.
  • Supported product marketing and implementation walkthrough planning.

Technical considerations

  • Define permissions between organizers and members at the API layer.
  • Keep group operations consistent through validation and service boundaries.
  • Move notifications into background jobs for responsive requests.
  • Treat organizer billing and entitlements as server-enforced rules.
