Who I am

About

I'm Ebuka Francis Mbanusi — a backend developer focused on Laravel (PHP 8.2+), MySQL/Redis, Docker, and AWS. I build robust APIs, payment flows & webhooks, background workers, and CI/CD pipelines with strong security and clean maintainable code.
